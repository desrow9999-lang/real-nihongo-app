Convenience Store

📖 Textbook: 「温めますか？」
Atatame masu ka? (Would you like it warmed up?)

🗣️ Real Shop: 「お弁当温めますか〜？」
Obento atatame masu ka~?

💡 Quick Reply: "Daijoubu desu" (No need) / "Onegai shimasu" (Yes, please)

Workplace / Job

📖 Textbook: 「分かりました」
Wakarimashita (I understood)

🗣️ Real Workplace: 「かしこまりました / 了解です！」
Kashikomarimashita (Polite) / Ryokai desu (Casual)

💡 Use "Kashikomarimashita" to bosses/customers, "Ryokai desu" to coworkers.

Izakaya / Restaurant

📖 Textbook: 「これをください」
Kore wo kudasai (Please give me this)

🗣️ Real Izakaya: 「とりあえず生（ビール）で！」
Toraiyazu nama de!

💡 The most famous phrase to order a draft beer first upon arriving!

The Magic Word: "Daijoubu" (大丈夫)

Depending on context, it means: 1. Yes / 2. No thanks / 3. I'm okay!

💡

All-in-One: "Sumimasen" (すみません)

Covers: "Excuse me", "Sorry", and "Thank you for your help".
